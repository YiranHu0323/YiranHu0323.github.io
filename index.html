Research

01

Autonomy-aware robot assistance

University of Notre Dame

Designing an Autonomy-POMDP that infers a user's latent autonomy from gaze observations and adapts robot assistance accordingly. I developed an LLM simulation with nine user personas and am extending this approach to real-world collaborative tasks.

02

Inclusive industrial robotics

University of Notre Dame

Studying how autistic workers perceive industrial robots as potential collaborators. Applied Self-Determination Theory to interviews and contributed to preliminary design principles for neuroaffirming human–robot interaction.

03

Anticipatory gaze & reward processing

University of Notre Dame

Analyzing preschoolers' anticipatory gaze and attention, including eye-tracking feature extraction, data quality assessment, and behavioral analysis pipelines.

04

Computer vision for physical tasks

Columbia University

Worked on automatic robotic suturing assessment with Video Swin Transformers and technical-skill prediction, alongside single-view 4D reconstruction using point clouds and learned geometric representations.
